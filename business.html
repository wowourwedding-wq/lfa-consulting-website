Non-profits & churches

Register once, register right

A non-profit needs three registrations to receive donations and issue tax certificates: an NPC at CIPC, an NPO at the Department of Social Development, and PBO / Section 18A status at SARS. We draft one founding document that satisfies all three, so nothing has to be redone later.
