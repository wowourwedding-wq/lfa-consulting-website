About LFA Consulting

Helping you stay compliant, organised & financially confident.

A South African accounting, tax, advisory and immigration consultancy built on clear pricing, personal service and doing things properly.

LFA Consulting is a South African firm helping businesses and individuals navigate accounting, tax, compliance and immigration with confidence. We believe good advice should be clear, honest and reachable — no jargon, no hidden fees, and an advisor who actually answers the phone.

From registering a new company and keeping it SARS-compliant, to filing your personal tax return or planning a move abroad, we handle the detail so you can focus on what matters. We serve clients nationwide and work remotely, which keeps our service personal and our pricing fair.

Meet the founder

Lilian Ngubane is the Founder & Principal of LFA Consulting — a qualified Management Accountant with a Master's in Business Leadership (MBL) and a background in CIMA studies. She founded LFA to give South African businesses and individuals the kind of accountable, straight-talking financial and compliance support that larger firms often reserve for their biggest clients.

What we stand for

Our promise is simple: keep your affairs compliant, organised and financially confident. We're transparent about what things cost, honest about what's realistic, and committed to getting the details right — because in compliance, the details are everything.
